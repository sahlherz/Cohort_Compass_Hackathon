# One-paragraph pitch (mapped to the judging criteria)

**CohortCompass turns what Tippie students and alumni know into the data the next student needs.** GPP's real constraint is that a few staff can't advise thousands of alumni, and today's review sites can't help, because vague free text can't be searched, compared or personalized. So we built an AI interviewer that runs one question at a time. It pulls structure out of a messy story, asks only for what's missing, and requires evidence for any 1-or-5 rating. The result is validated, versioned records, and those records power course pages, real events ranked with a reason, "what it took" career paths, and a Q&A that cites every claim or says "not enough data" (**usefulness**: Maya, Daniel or Thursday's mystery student all go from resume to a personalized plan in minutes). The **AI skills** are layered. A cheap model handles parsing, extraction and moderation, and a strong model handles interviewing and cited synthesis. The schema is the referee, citations are checked, and a scripted fallback means the demo never dead-ends. Everything is exposed as a standards-based MCP server with elicitation, resources, prompts and a portable Career Passport, so any AI host or campus system can plug in (**feasibility and scalability**: multi-tenant by campus and program, config as data, storage behind an adapter). It's built on real Tippie courses and events and synthetic people, with an "Under the hood" panel showing the exact tool call behind every click (**how it looks and process**). And it takes a clear position on GPP's open questions. The agent collects opt-in, structured knowledge. The AI drafts, but GPP staff own moderation, sensitive topics and any access to student records.
